# Decision Memo: Which AI — Shadow AI Discovery for SMB, Sold Through MSPs

## Verdict: PIVOT

**Score: 43/100** | Confidence: medium

> Competitor data is incomplete. ShadowLock and Lavawall withhold their rate cards, so the price you would be quoted against is unknown, and the category has almost no review corpus — the leading pure-play carries two G2 reviews. Retention is benchmarked, not observed.

---

## Why This Score

The market is real and the product is buildable. Two facts found since the August candidate score decide the outcome anyway. Microsoft now bundles Purview into M365 Business Premium, so adequate discovery costs a Microsoft-native client nothing extra. And two vendors already sell per-device shadow-AI discovery to MSPs. Competition scored 22, the lowest in this project and low enough to trigger the killer-dimension penalty. Meanwhile you have no route to this buyer.

## Top 3 Strengths

1. **Demand** (64/100): Clients raise AI controls themselves during SOC 2 audits and insurance renewals. Unauthorised AI use hit 68% of employees in early 2026, up from 41% in 2023, and one organisation in five has been breached through it.
2. **Founder-market fit** (58/100): Your SOC-analyst background maps directly onto risk classification, in a category that is fundamentally a trust sale.
3. **Monetization** (55/100): Two channels clear 3:1 with two-month payback, and per-device overage grows revenue with an MSP's client count.

## Top 3 Risks

1. **Competition** (22/100): Saturation scored a maximum 15 of 15. Microsoft bundles this into the licence your end clients already own; ShadowLock and Lavawall hold the MSP per-device position. *If ignored:* you build for six months against a free-at-the-margin default, differentiated only by a price two competitors already advertise.
2. **Distribution** (32/100): You have no MSP relationships, and the partner programmes that would scale this require SOC 2 Type II and ISO 27001. *If ignored:* you discover the channel is closed after the build instead of before.
3. **Retention** (48/100): Every renewal invites the question of why the client pays separately for something Purview covers. *If ignored:* you fight churn driven entirely by a competitor's bundling decision.

## Riskiest Assumption

> "MSPs will buy a standalone shadow-AI SKU from an unknown solo vendor, rather than waiting for the platform they already resell to ship the same thing."

**Test it before writing code.** Fourteen days, under $50. Contact 25 MSP owners as a practitioner asking a research question, not as a vendor. Ask whether a client has raised AI controls recently, then the real question: at $2 per device, white-labelled, would you buy now or wait for Huntress, ThreatLocker or Cynomi to add it? **Pass: at least 8 of 25 say buy now, and 2 put a card on file for a $199/month pilot.**

## Pre-mortem: If This Fails in 12 Months

1. **Purview was good enough.** Deals died on "our Microsoft licence covers that", and the nuanced answer about non-Microsoft coverage lost to a budget question.
2. **The channel never opened.** Partner programmes needed certifications you could not fund, MSP communities banned you for selling, and content never outranked six publishers with paid teams.
3. **An incumbent shipped it.** Cynomi was already multi-tenant and already publishing on MSP AI governance. A module took them one quarter, to MSPs they already bill.

## Devil's Advocate

1. **The demand data is the strongest in this project and I am discounting it on channel grounds.** Businesses have been built into worse markets with better hustle.
2. **Two competitors withholding rate cards may mean no traction, not ownership.** Harmonic's two reviews suggest nobody here has found a repeatable buyer, which is an opening as much as a warning.
3. **Bundling has been survived before.** Defender did not kill endpoint security; it killed the weak and pushed the rest up-market.

Each objection argues the market is attackable. None argues that you can reach it at 15 hours a week with no channel.

## What To Do Now

**Run the 25-MSP test this fortnight. Write no code.** Under $50 and two weeks against a decision worth six months. Your constraints make this clear-cut: at 15 hours a week, under $100 a month, with low risk tolerance, you cannot fund a slow channel build, and the fast channels here are closed or hostile.

**Kill criteria:** 15 or more of 25 say they would wait for an existing vendor. That means the outcome belongs to someone else's roadmap.

## If That Doesn't Work

Take the same capability to the buyer you can reach — the AI automation agencies in your inner circle, who deploy AI into client environments with no platform vendor waiting to solve this for them.

# Which AI — Shadow AI Discovery Priced for SMB, Sold Through MSPs

## The Problem

A 60-person company has no idea that its sales team pastes customer lists into ChatGPT, that finance uploads contracts to a free PDF-summariser nobody vetted, and that an engineer wired a Claude API key into a script on his laptop. There is no policy, no training, and no visibility — and the first time anyone finds out is during a SOC 2 audit, an insurance renewal questionnaire, or a breach.

The company's MSP would happily solve this. It is exactly the kind of recurring, high-margin service MSPs want. But every tool that does it is priced and operationally calibrated for a security team at a 500+ employee organisation. The MSP's client has 60 employees and no security team at all.

**Emotional trigger:** exposure without agency — knowing something is wrong, being asked to attest that it isn't, and having no instrument to look with.

**Audience vocabulary:**
- "shadow AI discovery"
- "what AI tools are my employees using"
- "AI usage policy for small business"
- "SOC 2 AI controls"
- "our cyber insurance is asking about AI"

## Market Signal Evidence

- **Web Search (scale of problem):** 65% of AI tools operate without IT approval. Breaches involving shadow AI cost an average of **$670,000 more**.
- **Web Search (buying trigger):** Clients are now the ones asking, driven by SOC 2 audits, HIPAA reviews, and **cyber insurance renewals**. The motion is responsive, not evangelistic — the single most valuable property a security product can have.
- **Web Search (the pricing hole):** Enterprise shadow-AI platforms are explicitly "priced and operationally calibrated for security teams at 500+ employee organisations," while "SMBs and their MSPs need discovery, risk classification, compliance reporting, and governance workflows in a single, manageable package." That is a stated, unmet requirement in a buyer's guide.
- **Web Search (channel):** 48% of MSPs rank AI/automation as top client need for 2026; only 13% monetise it. 92% see growth from client AI interest.

**Trend velocity:** rising

## App Concept

Which AI discovers every AI tool in use across a small company — via browser telemetry, DNS, OAuth grants, and expense data — classifies each by risk, and produces the policy and evidence pack the client's auditor or insurer is asking for. It is sold to MSPs, priced per endpoint like everything else they resell, and lives inside their existing service catalogue.

**App category:** SaaS/AI discovery and governance, MSP-channel distributed

## Key Features

1. **Multi-signal discovery** — OAuth grants, DNS queries, browser extension, and expense-data ingestion. No single signal catches everything; OAuth alone misses free tools used without sign-in.
2. **Risk classification** — Each discovered tool scored on data handling, training-on-input policy, jurisdiction, and vendor security posture. Turns a raw list into a decision.
3. **Policy generation and attestation** — Generates an AI usage policy, distributes it, and records employee acknowledgement. The attestation record is what the auditor actually wants to see.
4. **Compliance evidence pack** — Pre-formatted for SOC 2 and standard cyber-insurance questionnaires.
5. **MSP multi-tenant console** — One pane across all client tenants, per-endpoint billing, white-label reporting.

## Key Differentiator

The wedge is price and packaging, not technology — and that is a weaker moat than it sounds. The honest differentiator is that incumbents *cannot profitably* serve a 60-seat client through a channel partner: their pricing floor ($750/month under 150 accounts) and their assumption of an in-house security team both break at that size. Going down-market is a real structural constraint for them, not a matter of will.

The risk is that this is a well-known gap, not a hidden one. **ShadowLock is already publishing MSP-targeted AI-governance content**, which means at least one competitor has identified the same channel and is building an audience in it. This candidate is entering a race, not an empty field.

## Monetization Evidence

- Nudge Security: $5/active user/month for 150–2,500 accounts; $750/month under 150 accounts. A direct and well-documented price anchor.
- Shadow AI detection for 100–1,000 employee orgs: €8,000–€80,000/yr.
- MSP resale precedent: DNSFilter MSP plans from $150/month; Control D at $2/endpoint/month for SMB.
